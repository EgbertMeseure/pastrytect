Cutters in a row share one grip plate, so they print and cut as one piece. Without a rim they are still joined by a connecting bridge. The cutter prints rim-down and is flipped to use; asymmetric shapes (heart, egg, shield, cloud) are mirrored in the print view. Use “in use” to check.
